# AITM-SMB Anti-Patterns

**Status:** Informative ([`NORMATIVE_INDEX.md`](../NORMATIVE_INDEX.md) tier 13). Patterns a review should reject; the rules they break are normative in [`STANDARD.md`](../STANDARD.md) §3.

## Tool-first transformation
"We bought an AI assistant license; now define the transformation."

## Use-case theater
A long list of AI ideas without capability or outcome linkage.

## Automation of waste
Automating a broken process instead of redesigning it.

## Autonomous-by-default
Giving an agent authority because the model can technically act.

## Architecture cosplay
Producing diagrams with no decisions, constraints, ownership, or verification.

## Vendor-defined target state
Letting a cloud or SaaS product determine the operating model.

## Metric substitution
Using token counts, prompts, or AI adoption as proof of business value.

## Big-bang transformation
Attempting to reach the full target state without transition states and evidence gates.

## Hidden assumptions
Presenting inference as fact.

## Framework bureaucracy
Producing artifacts that do not change a decision.
