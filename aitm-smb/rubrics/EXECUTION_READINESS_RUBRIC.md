# Execution Readiness Rubric

## Initiative

- Is the Initiative bounded?
- Is ownership explicit?
- Is the behavior change explicit?

## Pilot

- Is the hypothesis explicit?
- Is baseline known?
- Are success and stop criteria pre-defined?
- Is scope bounded?
- Is rollback/recovery defined?

## Evaluation

- Are business and AI evaluations separate?
- Are thresholds explicit?
- Are datasets representative?
- Is attribution uncertainty visible?

## Operations

- Is observability active?
- Is incident ownership explicit?
- Is support defined?
- Is cost observable?

## Governance

- Are authority boundaries explicit?
- Is change control defined?
- Can authority be reduced?
- Are review triggers explicit?

## Adoption

- Are affected roles known?
- Are new behaviors and skills explicit?
- Can manual bypass be measured?

## Value

- Is there a baseline?
- Can capability effect be measured?
- Can economic effect be estimated?
- Is sustainability checked?
