# ADR-0002 — Outcome Before Technology

## Status
Accepted

## Date
Not recorded; accepted no later than the 1.0.0 release.

## Context
AI transformation is frequently started from a tool, a vendor offer, or a mandate to adopt AI rather than from a business result. Such starting points are means, not Outcomes ([`CORE_MODEL.md`](../CORE_MODEL.md) §1).

## Decision
AITM-SMB begins with measurable business outcomes and constraints before technology selection.

## Consequences
- technology remains subordinate to system design;
- AI can be rejected when unnecessary;
- vendor lock-in is delayed until justified;
- transformation success can be evaluated against business evidence.
