# ADR-0001 — Business Capability Is the Primary Transformation Unit

## Status
Accepted

## Date
Not recorded; accepted no later than the 1.0.0 release.

## Context
AI transformation is frequently framed as a portfolio of AI use cases.

## Decision
AITM-SMB uses the business capability as the primary unit of transformation.

## Consequences
- AI is evaluated in the context of the complete business system.
- Multiple AI interventions may exist inside one capability.
- Non-AI redesign remains visible.
- Outcome linkage becomes easier to preserve.
