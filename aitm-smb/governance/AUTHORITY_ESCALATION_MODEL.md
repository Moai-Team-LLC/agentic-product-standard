# Authority Escalation Model

## 1. Purpose

AITM-SMB increases AI authority only after demonstrated evidence.

---

## 2. Authority promotion path

```text
Suggest
→ Draft
→ Execute with approval
→ Execute within policy
→ Pursue bounded objective
```

---

## 3. Promotion criteria

Before increasing authority verify:

```text
task quality stable
failure modes understood
observability sufficient
policy enforcement sufficient
recovery tested
permissions bounded
incident rate acceptable
business value demonstrated
```

---

## 4. Demotion

Authority MUST be reducible.

Triggers may include:

```text
quality degradation
unexpected cost
new failure class
policy change
knowledge drift
model/provider change
security incident
```

---

## 5. Rule

Autonomy is a revocable operating privilege, not a permanent maturity achievement.
