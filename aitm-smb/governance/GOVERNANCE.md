# AITM-SMB Governance Model

For every material AI-enabled capability define:

```yaml
governance:
  owner:
  business_outcome:
  ai_role:
  human_role:
  data_access:
  tool_access:
  permissions:
  prohibited_actions:
  approval_required:
  escalation_conditions:
  audit_evidence:
  evaluation_method:
  failure_mode:
  recovery_path:
  economic_limit:
```

## Minimum governance questions

1. What may AI see?
2. What may AI infer?
3. What may AI recommend?
4. What may AI change?
5. What requires human approval?
6. When must execution stop?
7. What evidence must be retained?
8. How is quality evaluated?
9. Who owns failures?
10. What is the cost boundary?
