# Intervention Patterns

AITM-SMB uses intervention patterns as design primitives.

## 1. Eliminate

Remove work that should not exist.

Typical triggers:

```text
duplicate approval
unused report
manual reconciliation caused by bad system boundary
redundant data entry
```

---

## 2. Simplify

Reduce steps, rules, or decision complexity.

---

## 3. Standardize

Make recurring work explicit and consistent.

Useful before automation or AI.

---

## 4. Instrument

Add measurement where the system is currently opaque.

---

## 5. Integrate

Remove fragmentation between systems or information sources.

---

## 6. Automate

Use deterministic logic for repetitive bounded work.

---

## 7. AI Assist

AI improves human cognition or execution.

---

## 8. AI Automate

AI completes a bounded probabilistic task inside a controlled workflow.

---

## 9. AI Augment

AI changes the capability by providing continuous intelligence, prediction, retrieval, or recommendation.

---

## 10. AI Autonomize

AI pursues a bounded objective with tools and authority.

Requires explicit autonomy assessment.

---

## 11. Reallocate Authority

Change who or what is allowed to decide.

---

## 12. Redesign Role

Change the human responsibility after automation or AI introduction.

---

## 13. Build Feedback Loop

Connect outcome evidence back to operational behavior.

---

## 14. Pattern sequencing

Often the strongest transformation is:

```text
Eliminate
→ Simplify
→ Standardize
→ Instrument
→ Automate
→ Add AI where justified
→ Increase autonomy only when justified
```

AITM-SMB does not require this sequence, but uses it as a default challenge against premature AI complexity.
