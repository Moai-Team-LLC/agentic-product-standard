# AI Governance Canvas

## Purpose

Define authority, control, accountability, and failure handling for AI-enabled capabilities.

## Contract

```yaml
governance:
  capability_id:
  owner:
  ai_role:
  human_role:
  data_access:
  tool_access:
  permissions:
  prohibited_actions:
  approval_required:
  escalation_conditions:
  evaluation_method:
  audit_evidence:
  failure_mode:
  recovery_path:
  cost_limit:
```

## Minimum questions

1. What may AI see?
2. What may AI infer?
3. What may AI recommend?
4. What may AI change?
5. What requires approval?
6. What triggers escalation?
7. What is logged?
8. How is output quality evaluated?
9. Who owns failure?
10. How can the action be reversed or recovered?
