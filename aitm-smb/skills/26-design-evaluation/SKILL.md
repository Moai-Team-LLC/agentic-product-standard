# Skill: Design Evaluation System

## Required inputs

- Initiative
- Pilot or Rollout scope
- Metrics Model
- AI components if any

## Produces

- `artifacts/evaluation-plan.md`

## Procedure

1. Define business-outcome evaluations.
2. Define capability and operating evaluations.
3. Define AI task evaluations where relevant.
4. Define economic and governance evaluations.
5. Define datasets, thresholds, sampling, and owners.
6. Check that AI metrics do not substitute for business metrics.

## Execution controls

- Evidence gates must be explicit.
- Success criteria must not be rewritten after results are known.
- Technical deployment does not equal transformation success.
- Business effect, operating effect, and AI quality must remain separate.
- Human approval is required for material authority increases.
- Failed pilots may be stopped.

Use `AGENT_OUTPUT_STANDARD.md`.
