# Skill: Design Change Adoption

## Required inputs

- Target Operating Architecture
- Role transitions
- Rollout Plan

## Produces

- `artifacts/adoption-plan.md`

## Procedure

1. Identify affected roles and behaviors.
2. Identify required skills and training.
3. Identify incentive or metric changes.
4. Identify trust and workflow-fit risks.
5. Define support and feedback channels.
6. Define adoption metrics.

## Execution controls

- Evidence gates must be explicit.
- Success criteria must not be rewritten after results are known.
- Technical deployment does not equal transformation success.
- Business effect, operating effect, and AI quality must remain separate.
- Human approval is required for material authority increases.
- Failed pilots may be stopped.
