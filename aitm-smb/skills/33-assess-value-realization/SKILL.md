# Skill: Assess Value Realization

## Required inputs

- baseline
- post-rollout evidence
- economic data
- business metrics

## Produces

- `artifacts/value-realization-report.md`

## Procedure

1. Compare business Outcome against baseline and target.
2. Trace technical/process change to behavior and capability effect.
3. Estimate economic effect.
4. Assess attribution confidence.
5. Assess sustainability.
6. Classify value result.

## Execution controls

- Evidence gates must be explicit.
- Success criteria must not be rewritten after results are known.
- Technical deployment does not equal transformation success.
- Business effect, operating effect, and AI quality must remain separate.
- Human approval is required for material authority increases.
- Failed pilots may be stopped.

Use `AGENT_OUTPUT_STANDARD.md`.
