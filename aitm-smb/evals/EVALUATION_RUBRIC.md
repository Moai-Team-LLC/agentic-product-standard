# AITM-SMB Evaluation Rubric

Use this rubric to review an engagement, artifact set, or agent output.

## A. Outcome integrity
- Is transformation linked to measurable business outcomes?
- Are baseline and target states explicit?

## B. System integrity
- Are capabilities connected to people, process, decisions, information, and applications?
- Are causal claims distinguished from symptoms?

## C. AI necessity
- Is AI justified?
- Was a simpler deterministic/process alternative considered?

## D. Architecture integrity
- Is target behavior defined before vendors?
- Are authority and trust boundaries explicit?

## E. Evidence integrity
- Can material conclusions be traced to evidence?
- Are assumptions visible?

## F. Transformation integrity
- Is the roadmap incremental and testable?
- Are transition states reversible where practical?

## G. Governance integrity
- Are AI permissions, approval gates, escalation, and ownership explicit?

## H. Measurement integrity
- Are business outcomes measured after implementation?

## Failure condition

A polished artifact that cannot answer these questions is not a valid AITM-SMB output.
