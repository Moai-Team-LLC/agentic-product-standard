# Pilot Quality Rubric

A strong Pilot answers:

```text
What hypothesis is being tested?
What business behavior changes?
What scope is bounded?
What baseline exists?
What counts as success?
What counts as stop?
What evidence will be collected?
How can failure be contained?
Who owns the decision after the pilot?
```

A demo is not a pilot.

A pilot without pre-defined success criteria is not valid evidence.
