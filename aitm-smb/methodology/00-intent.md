# Phase 0 — Intent

## Objective

Define why transformation is being considered and what measurable outcomes matter.

## Required inputs

- approved upstream artifacts;
- direct evidence where available;
- explicit assumptions where evidence is absent.

## Activities

1. identify business problem or opportunity;
2. define baseline;
3. define desired outcome;
4. define owner;
5. identify constraints;
6. define decision horizon;
7. establish success/failure criteria.

## Outputs

- Transformation Intent
- initial constraints
- baseline metrics
- Decision & Assumption Log

## Decision gates

Stop if there is no meaningful business outcome or accountable owner.

## Validation

- output is consistent with `STANDARD.md`;
- facts and assumptions are separated;
- every material conclusion is traceable to evidence or explicitly labeled inference;
- unresolved critical uncertainty is visible.
