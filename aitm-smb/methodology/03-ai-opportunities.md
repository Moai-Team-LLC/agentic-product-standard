# Phase 3 — Intervention & AI Opportunity Design

## Objective

Determine which changes could close diagnosed capability Gaps and whether AI is justified.

## Required inputs

- intervention-ready Gaps;
- validated causes or accepted hypotheses;
- constraints;
- evidence.

## Method

Use:

1. `design/INTERVENTION_PATTERNS.md`
2. `diagnostics/AI_SUITABILITY.md`
3. `diagnostics/AUTONOMY_SUITABILITY.md`
4. `DECISION_MODEL.md`

## Intervention sequence challenge

For each Gap consider:

```text
Eliminate
→ Simplify
→ Standardize
→ Instrument
→ Integrate
→ Automate
→ AI Assist
→ AI Automate
→ AI Augment
→ AI Autonomize
```

The sequence is a challenge heuristic, not a mandatory pipeline.

## Activities

1. generate multiple intervention candidates;
2. include non-AI alternatives;
3. assess AI suitability;
4. classify AI fit A–E;
5. if autonomy is proposed, assess autonomy separately;
6. define required context, actions, permissions, verification, and risks;
7. record uncertainty and economic assumptions.

## Outputs

- Intervention & Intervention Map;
- AI Suitability Assessments;
- Autonomy Assessments where relevant;
- rejected alternatives with rationale.

## Exit condition

An AI intervention is ready for prioritization only when:

```text
Gap trace exists
AND AI need is justified
AND simpler alternatives were considered
AND verification is plausible
AND authority assumptions are explicit
```
