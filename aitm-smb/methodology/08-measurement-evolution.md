# Phase 8 — Measurement & Evolution

## Objective

Determine whether the transformation created sustained business value and evolve the system based on evidence.

## Required inputs

- baseline metrics;
- pilot/rollout evidence;
- operating data;
- AI evaluations where relevant;
- cost data;
- incident history.

## Method

Use:

1. `evaluation/EVALUATION_SYSTEM.md`
2. `evaluation/AI_EVALS.md`
3. `measurement/VALUE_REALIZATION.md`
4. `measurement/BENEFIT_EVIDENCE_CHAIN.md`
5. `operations/INCIDENT_MODEL.md`

## Activities

1. evaluate business Outcomes;
2. evaluate Capability performance;
3. evaluate operating performance;
4. evaluate AI quality where relevant;
5. evaluate economics;
6. assess attribution confidence;
7. assess value sustainability;
8. incorporate incident and exception evidence;
9. update diagnosis, Capability Target State, Target Operating Architecture, or Roadmap where evidence disproves assumptions;
10. decide promote, revise, scale, reduce authority, or stop.

## Outputs

- Transformation Scorecard;
- Value Realization Report;
- updated Evaluation records;
- revised assumptions and decisions;
- architecture/roadmap updates where required.

## Exit condition

An Initiative is value-confirmed only when:

```text
business effect evidenced
AND capability effect evidenced
AND economics acceptable
AND governance remains effective
AND effect is sufficiently sustained
```
