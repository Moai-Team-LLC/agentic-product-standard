# Phase 1 — Current System

## Objective

Build an evidence-based model of how the business currently produces value.

## Required inputs

- approved upstream artifacts;
- direct evidence where available;
- explicit assumptions where evidence is absent.

## Activities

1. map value streams;
2. map capabilities;
3. connect roles, decisions, processes, data, knowledge, and applications;
4. identify handoffs and dependencies;
5. record failure modes and workarounds.

## Outputs

- Business System Map
- Capability Map
- evidence inventory

## Decision gates

Do not infer target-state solutions in the current-state model.

## Validation

- output is consistent with `STANDARD.md`;
- facts and assumptions are separated;
- every material conclusion is traceable to evidence or explicitly labeled inference;
- unresolved critical uncertainty is visible.
