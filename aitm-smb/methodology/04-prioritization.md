# Phase 4 — Prioritization

## Objective

Choose transformation initiatives with the highest justified leverage.

## Required inputs

- approved upstream artifacts;
- direct evidence where available;
- explicit assumptions where evidence is absent.

## Activities

Evaluate:
- business value;
- frequency/volume;
- strategic relevance;
- data/knowledge readiness;
- implementation complexity;
- operational risk;
- change burden;
- reversibility;
- time-to-evidence.

## Outputs

- Prioritization Matrix
- selected initiatives
- rejected/deferred initiatives with rationale

## Decision gates

Human approval is required before an initiative enters the roadmap.

## Validation

- output is consistent with `STANDARD.md`;
- facts and assumptions are separated;
- every material conclusion is traceable to evidence or explicitly labeled inference;
- unresolved critical uncertainty is visible.
