# Change Adoption Model

## 1. Purpose

Transformation fails when the technical system changes but human behavior does not.

AITM-SMB treats adoption as architecture execution.

---

## 2. Adoption dimensions

```text
Role clarity
Skill readiness
Trust
Workflow fit
Incentives
Manager behavior
Exception handling
Feedback
Support
```

---

## 3. Adoption record

Record contract: [`artifacts/adoption-plan.md`](../artifacts/adoption-plan.md).

An adoption record states, per Capability, the affected roles and behavior changes, required skills and training, incentive changes, trust and workflow-fit risks, support model, feedback channel, and adoption metrics. It SHOULD address every §2 dimension that the change touches.

---

## 4. Adoption metrics

Examples:

```text
target workflow usage
override rate
manual bypass rate
time-to-competence
support requests
correction rate
exception handling quality
```

Adoption metrics do not substitute for business Outcome metrics.

---

## 5. Resistance as evidence

Resistance MAY indicate:

```text
poor communication
bad incentives
real workflow degradation
loss of control
unresolved risk
incorrect target-state design
```

Do not treat all resistance as a people problem.
